Privacy Policy

ትግርኛ ነንብብ – Tigrigna Reader · Brhan Tech · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how the app ትግርኛ ነንብብ (Tigrigna Reader), made by Brhan Tech, handles information. In short: we do not collect, store on our own servers, sell or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

ብሓጺሩ፦ እዚ ኣፕ ዝኾነ ውልቃዊ ሓበሬታ ኣይእክብን፣ ኣይሸይጥን፣ ምስ ካልኦት ኣይካፈልን። ኩሉ ኣብ ተሌፎንኩም ጥራይ ይዕቀብ።

Information kept on your device

  • Reading progress: which readings you opened, quiz scores, stars, review dates and the page where you stopped.
  • Your name, if you enter one: used only to print it on your certificates (ምስክር ወረቐት).
  • Purchase status: whether the full library has been unlocked.

This information is saved only in the app's storage on your phone or tablet. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app, or using ኩሉ ደጊምኩም ጀምሩ in the app, removes your progress.

Things we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party data collection tools.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a reminder on your own device after a week without reading. The reminder is created and shown by your phone; no information is sent anywhere. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The first 5 readings are free. The one-time unlock is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from the store that the unlock was bought, so it can open all readings. Before a purchase, the app shows a short parent check.

Sharing certificates and rating the app

When you tap ኣካፍሉ ወይ ኣቐምጡ, the certificate image is passed to your phone's own share menu, and you choose where it goes. When you tap ⭐ ገምግሙና or the rating box appears, the rating is handled by Google Play or the App Store.

Children

The app is made for readers of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect any information from children. Purchases are behind a parent check.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
